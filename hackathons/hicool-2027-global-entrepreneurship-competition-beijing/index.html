HICOOL 2027 Global Entrepreneurship Competition (Beijing)

HICOOL 2027 全球创业大赛（北京）

HICOOL 2027 Global Entrepreneurship Competition (Beijing) is a in person hackathon (Beijing, China) on 1 August 2027, run by HICOOL (Shunyi, Beijing). Theme: Open to founders worldwide; 10,000+ projects from 141 countries entered in 2026. Prizes: 2026: 200 awards, CNY 200K–2M cash each, plus residency, insurance and incubation support.
