Win in Suzhou Global Startup Competition 2027

“赢在苏州·创赢未来”全球创业大赛 2027

Win in Suzhou Global Startup Competition 2027 is a hybrid hackathon (Suzhou, China; qualifying rounds elsewhere (incl. Hong Kong)) on 1 March 2027, run by Suzhou Dushu Lake Science & Education Innovation District. Theme: Seed group: individuals or teams not yet registered in Suzhou. Prizes: 2026: RMB 100K / 80K / 50K; projects that move to Suzhou get 2 free desks for up to 3 years.
