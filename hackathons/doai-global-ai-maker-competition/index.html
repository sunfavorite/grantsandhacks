DOAI Global AI Maker Competition

DOAI 大会 · 全球 AI 创客大赛

DOAI Global AI Maker Competition is a in person hackathon (Shanghai, China) on 5 December 2026 to 6 December 2026, run by DoBe Group × Atom Commune. Theme: Two tracks: real enterprise problems (FDE) and free creative (AI infra, apps, hardware). Prizes: RMB 150K prize money plus a flexible-office pass and token credits.
