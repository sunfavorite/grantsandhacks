4th CUHK-Shenzhen “Shenxianhu” International Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition

第四届港中大（深圳）“神仙湖”国际创新创业大赛

4th CUHK-Shenzhen “Shenxianhu” International Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition is a in person hackathon (Shenzhen, China) on 10 January 2027, run by CUHK-Shenzhen (title sponsor: Today International). Theme: Open to companies, universities, research institutes and teams; past entries came from 29 countries/regions. Prizes: RMB 1M prize pool; up to RMB 5M from the CUHK-SZ seed fund (investment); up to 12 months free incubation.
