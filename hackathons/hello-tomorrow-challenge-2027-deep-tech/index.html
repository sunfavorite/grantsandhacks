Hello Tomorrow Challenge 2027 (deep tech)

Hello Tomorrow Challenge 2027（深科技创业大赛）

Hello Tomorrow Challenge 2027 (deep tech) is a online application hackathon (Finals in Amsterdam; open worldwide) on 2 June 2027 to 3 June 2027, run by Hello Tomorrow. Theme: Deep tech startups (pre-seed to Series D, team of 2+); research/PhD teams can apply before incorporating. Prizes: Grand Prize: €200K investment from Deepbright Ventures (equity); applying is free and takes no equity.
