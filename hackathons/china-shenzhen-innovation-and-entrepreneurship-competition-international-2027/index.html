China Shenzhen Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition · International (2027)

中国深圳创新创业大赛 · 国际赛（2027）

China Shenzhen Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition · International (2027) is a hybrid hackathon (Overseas rounds + finals in Shenzhen) on 1 June 2027, run by Shenzhen Government. Theme: Recruits overseas projects (2026 included a Stockholm round); winners who start a company in Shenzhen within 5 years can apply for the returnee start-up grant. Prizes: Linked to Shenzhen's returnee start-up grant: up to RMB 1M.
