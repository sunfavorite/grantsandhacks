Chuangke China SME Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition (12th, 2027)

“创客中国”中小企业创新创业大赛（第 12 届，2027）

Chuangke China SME Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition (12th, 2027) is a in person hackathon (Provincial and city rounds (e.g. Shenzhen)) on 1 May 2027, run by MIIT and local industry bureaus. Theme: Company group and startup group (maker teams can enter). Prizes: 2026 Shenzhen leg: RMB 10K–50K cash plus guaranteed loans. Who can apply: Usually need local registration, or enter as a maker team.
