China Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition (16th, 2027)

中国创新创业大赛（第十六届，2027）

China Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition (16th, 2027) is a in person hackathon (Regional rounds across China, national finals) on 1 June 2027, run by MIIT Torch High Technology Industry Development Center. Theme: One of China's largest tech startup competitions, with startup and growth groups by industry. Prizes: 1st–3rd prizes by industry, plus referrals to funds and bank loans. Who can apply: Non-listed tech companies registered in China with their own IP; no entry fee.
