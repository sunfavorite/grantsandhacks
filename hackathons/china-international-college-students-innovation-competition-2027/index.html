China International College Students' Innovation Competition 2027

中国国际大学生创新大赛 2027（原“互联网+”）

China International College Students' Innovation Competition 2027 is a hybrid hackathon (National, with an international track) on 10 August 2027, run by Ministry of Education and partners. Theme: Student and graduate startup projects; 600 finalist places for international projects in 2026. Prizes: Gold, silver, bronze awards (no cash amounts in the notice). Who can apply: Students or graduates aged 35 or under; international projects register via pilcchina.org.
