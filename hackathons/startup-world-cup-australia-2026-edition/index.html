Startup World Cup Australia (2026 edition)

Startup World Cup Australia（往届 2026）

Startup World Cup Australia (2026 edition) is a in person hackathon (Melbourne / Sydney / Queensland) on 14 May 2026 to 9 July 2026, run by Startup & Angels (global competition founded by Pegasus Tech Ventures). Theme: Startup pitch competition, any industry or stage, 10 pitch spots per city. Prizes: City winners: flights (United Airlines) + Silicon Valley week and a place in the global Grand Finale in San Francisco; the overall winner gets a US$1M investment (not cash).
