Shanghai Global Talents Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition (7th)

上海“海聚英才”全球创新创业大赛（第 7 届）

Shanghai Global Talents Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition (7th) is a hybrid hackathon (Shanghai, China; overseas zones include Oceania) on 7 April 2027, run by Shanghai Talent+. Theme: Seed-stage projects not registered in China can enter through the overseas zones. Prizes: 2026: participation subsidies CNY 5K–100K, plus loans and free office space (no equity).
