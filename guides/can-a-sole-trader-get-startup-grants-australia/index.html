Can an Australian sole trader get startup grants?

澳洲 Sole Trader（独资经营者）能申请创业补助吗？

Short answer: Mostly no. The main federal and NSW startup programs we track (R&D Tax Incentive, Industry Growth Program, CSIRO Kick-Start, NSW MVP Ventures) require a company with an ACN; individuals and sole traders are excluded. BRII is the exception: an individual can apply if they form an Australian company before signing the grant agreement. If you plan to apply for startup grants, registering a Pty Ltd company is usually the first step.
简要回答：大多数不行。我们收录的主要联邦和新州创业资助（R&D Tax Incentive、Industry Growth Program、CSIRO Kick-Start、NSW MVP Ventures）都要求是有 ACN 的公司，个人和 sole trader 不能申请。BRII 是例外：个人可以申请，但签资助协议前必须注册成立澳洲公司。打算申请创业资助的话，通常第一步是注册一家 Pty Ltd 公司。

Program by program

ProgramSole trader?What the official rules say
R&D Tax Incentive (federal)NoYou must be a corporation incorporated under Australian law (or a foreign resident company in some cases). The ATO says individuals are not eligible. You also need at least A$20,000 of eligible R&D spending in the year.
Industry Growth Program (federal)NoApplicants need an ABN, GST registration and must be a company incorporated in Australia, a co-operative or an incorporated trustee. Individuals and partnerships are excluded. As of 16 November 2026 the program is paused to new grant applications.
CSIRO Kick-Start (federal)NoYou must hold an ACN or ICN and be registered for GST. CSIRO's FAQ answers “I don't run my own company, am I eligible?” with “No”.
BRII (federal)Yes, with a conditionIndividuals and partnerships may apply, provided they form an Australian company before entering the grant agreement. Turnover must be under A$20 million.
NSW MVP Ventures (NSW)NoThe round guidelines list “an individual/sole trader”, partnerships and trusts under “who can't apply”. You must be a company incorporated in Australia (or an Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander corporation) headquartered in NSW.

What this means if you are a sole trader today

  • Register a company before you need the money. Almost every program above checks your entity type at application time, and some (like the R&D Tax Incentive) only count spending incurred by the company.
  • Check the turnover and age tests. MVP Ventures caps turnover at A$400,000 in each of the three prior financial years and staff at 10 full-time equivalents. CSIRO Kick-Start wants turnover and operating spend under A$10 million or a business trading for under three years.
  • Plan for co-funding. Kick-Start must be matched dollar for dollar in cash, and the Industry Growth Program covers at most 50% of eligible spending.
  • Prizes and hackathons don't care about your structure. Competition prizes and hackathons are usually open to individuals, so they are a realistic source of non-dilutive money while you are still a sole trader. See our hackathon list.

Common questions

Can a sole trader claim the R&D Tax Incentive?

No. The ATO says you must be a corporation incorporated under Australian law, and individuals are not eligible for the R&D tax offset.

Can a sole trader apply for NSW MVP Ventures?

No. The NSW Government lists individuals and sole traders, partnerships and trusts as unable to apply. You need a company incorporated in Australia that is headquartered in NSW.

Is there any federal startup grant a sole trader can apply for?

The Business Research and Innovation Initiative (BRII) lets individuals apply, but they must form an Australian company before signing a grant agreement. It only runs when government agencies publish challenges.

Do I need GST registration?

For CSIRO Kick-Start and the Industry Growth Program, yes. Both require GST registration as well as a company structure.
