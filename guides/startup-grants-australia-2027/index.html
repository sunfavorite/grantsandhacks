What startup grants are available in Australia in 2027?

2027 年澳洲有哪些创业资助可以申请？

Short answer: Heading into 2027, the main equity-free options for an early-stage Australian startup are the R&D Tax Incentive (claimed every financial year), CSIRO Kick-Start (A$20k–60k, matched, applications all year) and, in NSW, MVP Ventures (A$20k–75k, next round announced as “opening soon”). The federal Industry Growth Program is paused to new grant applications, and BRII only opens when agencies publish challenges. All of them require a company.
简要回答：进入 2027 年，澳洲早期初创可申请的主要不占股资助有：R&D Tax Incentive（每个财年申报）、CSIRO Kick-Start（A$2–6 万，需配套，全年可申请），以及新州的 MVP Ventures（A$2–7.5 万，官网称下一轮“即将开放”）。联邦 Industry Growth Program 目前暂停受理新申请，BRII 只在政府部门发布挑战题目时开放。以上都要求申请方是公司。

Common questions

Is CSIRO Kick-Start open in 2027?

CSIRO says Kick-Start expressions of interest can be submitted all year round. Funding is A$20,000 to A$60,000 and must be matched in cash.

When does NSW MVP Ventures open next?

As of 12 November 2026 the NSW Government page says the next funding round will open soon, without a date. The previous round opened 9 March 2026 and closed 10 April 2026.

Is the Industry Growth Program accepting applications?

As of 16 November 2026, business.gov.au says the program remains paused to new grant applications.
