NSW Regional Event Fund (Incubator and Flagship streams)

NSW 区域活动基金 Regional Event Fund（Incubator / Flagship）

NSW Regional Event Fund (Incubator and Flagship streams) is a grant / funding program from Destination NSW. Area: Regional NSW (not Greater Sydney). Amount: Incubator up to A$20K; Flagship A$20K a year (A$30K a year for 3 years after three annual grants). No equity. Who can apply: Event owner with an ABN and A$20M public liability cover; event must be in one of 7 regional Destination Networks; excludes business events, one-off events and Greater Sydney.
