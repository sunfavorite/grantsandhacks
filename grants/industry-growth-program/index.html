Industry Growth Program

Industry Growth Program is a grant / funding program from Australian Government. Area: Australia-wide. Amount: Early-stage commercialisation A$50k–250k; commercialisation and growth A$100k–5m; covers up to 50% of eligible spending. Who can apply: Company incorporated in Australia (or co-operative / incorporated trustee) with an ABN and GST registration, turnover under A$20m in each of the past 3 years; individuals, partnerships and sole traders cannot apply.
