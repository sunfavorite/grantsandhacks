Shenzhen Returnee Start-up Grant

深圳市留学人员创业资助

Shenzhen Returnee Start-up Grant is a grant / funding program from Shenzhen Human Resources and Social Security Bureau. Area: Shenzhen, China. Amount: Three tiers: RMB 300K / 500K / 1M (no equity). Who can apply: 3+ years studying/working abroad, shares worth RMB 300K+; company registered in Shenzhen 6–48 months.
