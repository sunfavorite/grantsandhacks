CSIRO Kick-Start

CSIRO Kick-Start is a grant / funding program from CSIRO. Area: Australia-wide. Amount: A$20k–60k of R&D funding (as a CSIRO voucher), matched 1:1 in cash. Who can apply: Must hold an ACN or ICN and be GST registered; turnover and operating spend under A$10m, or trading under 3 years; sole traders are not eligible.
