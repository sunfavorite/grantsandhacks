NSW MVP Ventures Program

NSW MVP Ventures Program is a grant / funding program from Investment NSW (NSW Government). Area: Sydney / all of NSW. Amount: A$20k–50k; Stream 2 (majority women-owned/run, regional or First Nations) up to A$75k. Who can apply: Must be a company incorporated in Australia (or an Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander corporation), NSW HQ, owns IP, ≤10 FTE including founders, turnover ≤A$400k in each of the past 3 years; individuals / sole traders, partnerships and trusts cannot apply.
