Business Research and Innovation Initiative (BRII)

BRII 商业研究与创新计划

Business Research and Innovation Initiative (BRII) is a grant / funding program from Australian Government. Area: Australia-wide. Amount: Up to A$100k feasibility; up to A$1m proof of concept (by invitation). Who can apply: ABN and turnover under A$20m; individuals or partnerships can apply if they form an Australian company before signing the grant agreement.
