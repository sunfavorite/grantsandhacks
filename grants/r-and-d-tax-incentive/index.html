R&D Tax Incentive

R&D Tax Incentive 研发税收优惠

R&D Tax Incentive is a grant / funding program from Australian Government (ATO / AusIndustry). Area: Australia-wide. Amount: Loss-making companies get up to 43.5% back in cash. Who can apply: Must be a company incorporated in Australia; individuals and sole traders cannot claim; at least A$20,000 of eligible R&D spending a year.
