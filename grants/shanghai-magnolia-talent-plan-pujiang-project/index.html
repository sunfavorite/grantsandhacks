Shanghai Magnolia Talent Plan · Pujiang Project

上海白玉兰人才计划 · 浦江项目

Shanghai Magnolia Talent Plan · Pujiang Project is a grant / funding program from Shanghai Talent Work Bureau. Area: Shanghai, China. Amount: RMB 300K per person, RMB 500K per team (no equity). Who can apply: Aged 45 or under, full-time in Shanghai at least 9 months a year; applying unit must be registered in Shanghai.
