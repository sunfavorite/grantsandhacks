Cartier Women's Initiative (2028 edition)

卡地亚女性创业家奖 Cartier Women's Initiative（2028 届）

Cartier Women's Initiative (2028 edition) is a grant / funding program from Cartier Women's Initiative. Area: Global, with an Oceania regional award. Amount: US$30K / 60K / 100K per region, equity-free, plus an INSEAD programme. Who can apply: Revenue US$50K–5M, team of 5–250, under US$2M dilutive funding raised.
